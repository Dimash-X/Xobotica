# Xobotica
### Exploring Robotics Through Real-World Projects and 3D Simulation

Xobotica is a robotics project showcase and interactive simulation platform dedicated to presenting my engineering creations, exploring their capabilities, and developing their intelligence through experimentation.

The core idea behind Xobotica is to give everyone the opportunity to interact with my robots in virtual 3D environments. Visitors can experiment with different scenarios, create challenges, overcome obstacles, and help train and improve the AI systems controlling these robots.

**My goal is to turn robotics development into an open, interactive experience where anyone can contribute to making my robots smarter through virtual experimentation and AI training.**

The platform also allows others to discover my robots, understand how they are designed and built, and see how they operate in the real world.

## About

Every robot starts as an idea and becomes an engineering project through design, development, and experimentation.

Xobotica brings these projects together in one place, providing insights into their development, hardware, functionality, and real-world applications.

Beyond showcasing physical robots, the platform explores their virtual counterparts through 3D simulations. These environments allow people to interact with robots, test their capabilities, experiment with different conditions, and explore new ways to improve their behaviour.

## What You Can Explore

- **Robotics Projects** - Discover my robotic creations and engineering ideas.
- **Real-World Demonstrations** - See how the robots are built, operate, and perform in real-world conditions.
- **Interactive 3D Simulations** - Control and experiment with virtual versions of my robots.
- **AI Training & Development** - Participate in experiments aimed at improving robot intelligence and autonomous behaviour.
- **Testing & Challenges** - Create and explore different obstacles, tasks, and scenarios to test robot capabilities.
- **Development Process** - Learn about the hardware, software, design decisions, and technologies behind each project.

## Vision

To build an interactive robotics ecosystem where people from around the world can explore my engineering creations, experiment with them, and contribute to developing more intelligent robotic systems.

I envision a platform where robotics enthusiasts, developers, and curious minds can work with virtual robots, conduct experiments, test new ideas, and help train AI systems that may eventually control real-world machines.

**The ultimate goal is to connect human curiosity, collective experimentation, artificial intelligence, and real-world robotics.**

### DOGX - Miniature Quadrupedal Robot with a Custom AI Brain

DOGX is an upcoming personal robotics project focused on creating a highly compact, lightweight, four-legged robot with an entirely custom-developed AI brain.

The project is currently in its planning and development stage. My goal is to design and build the robot from the ground up, while independently developing and training its AI system rather than relying on ready-made solutions.

I want to make this development process interactive. Through the Xobotica platform, visitors will be able to explore DOGX in a virtual 3D environment, experiment with its movement, test different approaches, and help me develop and improve its AI brain. Together, we can experiment with reinforcement learning to teach DOGX how to walk, maintain balance, adapt to different surfaces, and navigate its surroundings.

**The ultimate goal is to turn DOGX into an intelligent, autonomous quadrupedal robot — with its own AI brain developed through experimentation and collaboration with the Xobotica community.**

- **Type:** Miniature Quadrupedal Robot
- **Status:** Planned / In Development
- **Design:** Custom compact and lightweight construction
- **Degrees of Freedom:** 12 (planned, 3 per leg)
- **AI Brain:** Custom AI system developed from scratch
- **Learning Approach:** Reinforcement Learning (RL)
- **Main Goals:** Autonomous walking, dynamic balance, environmental adaptation, and intelligent navigation
- **Community Involvement:** Interactive simulation, experimentation, and collaborative AI development
